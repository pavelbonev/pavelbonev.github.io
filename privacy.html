Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Applies to Euromondo for Android, for iPhone, the web app and this site.

In short: you sign in with Google or Apple, and we keep only the number that Google or Apple gives your account for Euromondo, not your name or e-mail. Besides that we keep your nickname and what you do in Euromondo: predictions, swarms, chat messages and your subscription. No ads, no tracking, no analytics. The server is ours, in Bulgaria.

Who is responsible

Euromondo is run by Pavel Bonev, a private individual in Bulgaria (“we”). He is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: pavelbonev@gmail.com.

What we keep, why and for how long

WhatWhyHow long
Your sign-in ID: the number Google or Apple gives your account for Euromondo. We do not ask Google or Apple for your name, e-mail or photo, and we do not keep them. To know that it is you when you sign in. Until you delete your account.
Your nickname, which you choose. Until you choose one, the app gives you a neutral one. Other players see it in the standings, in swarms and in chat. Until you delete your account.
Your predictions and when you gave them, your champion pick, and the points and statistics made from them, also for past tournaments. To play: points, standings and the history of past tournaments. Until you delete your account.
Swarms: the swarms you created (name, description, PIN) and the ones you joined, with the date. So that a swarm has its members and its own standings. Until you leave or delete the swarm, or delete your account.
Chat: the messages you send (direct messages and swarm chats), when you sent them, and which messages you have read. To deliver them and to show what is unread. Until you delete your account; after that they stay, shown as from “deleted user” (see below).
Your subscription: the store's order number and purchase token, the product, its status and dates, and the purchase record the store sends us. We never see your name, card or payment details. To check with Google Play or the App Store that your subscription is active, and to unlock swarms on all your devices. Until you delete your account.
Server logs: your IP address, the time and the address requested. To keep the service secure and to find errors. 30 days.
Backups of the database, on a separate disk in the same place as the server. So that nothing is lost if a disk fails. Daily copies for 30 days, monthly copies for 12 months.

On your device the app keeps your sign-in session (so that you stay signed in), your language and a copy of the match data. The web app keeps the same in your browser's local storage. We use no cookies of our own, and this site uses none at all.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions, and we do not sell it.

If you write to us by e-mail, we keep your message and your address to answer you, for up to a year after the last reply, or three years for a request about your rights, to show that we answered it. Our e-mail is in Gmail, so Google keeps it too.

Legal basis

  • Your account, predictions, swarms, chat and subscription: we need them to provide Euromondo to you (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR, a contract).
  • Server logs and backups: our legitimate interest in a secure service that does not lose data (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). You can object to it; see “Your rights”.

Who else sees your data

  • Other players see your nickname, your place and numbers in the standings (points, number of predictions, exact scores, outcomes, champion points) and which swarms you are in. They do not see your individual predictions: the app shows only how all players together predicted a match. The members of a swarm see its chat; a direct message is seen by the player you send it to.
  • Google: when you sign in with Google, Google tells us your sign-in ID and knows that you use Euromondo. Google Play handles the subscription purchase on Android. The web app loads the Google sign-in button from Google's servers, so Google sees your IP address and may use its own cookies. This is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: when you sign in with Apple, Apple tells us your sign-in ID. The App Store handles the purchase on iPhone. This is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Nobody else. The server is ours, at our home in Bulgaria: there is no hosting company, no analytics and no ad network. Your data stays in the European Union, except what Google and Apple handle themselves under their own policies.
  • Authorities, only when the law obliges us.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app or in the web app: open the menu with your nickname and choose Delete account. Step by step, also without the app: Delete your account.

This deletes your sign-in IDs, nickname, predictions (also from past tournaments), champion picks, statistics, swarm memberships and subscription records. A swarm you created passes to the member who joined it first; a swarm without other members is deleted. Your chat messages stay, so that the conversations still make sense, but they are shown as from “deleted user” and are no longer linked to you; if you want some of them removed, write to us. The deleted data disappears from the backups within 12 months.

Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription: cancel it in Google Play or in the settings of your Apple Account. Google and Apple keep their own purchase history.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the data we have about you and get a copy of it, also as a file you can take elsewhere (access and portability);
  • correct it (you can change your nickname in the app yourself);
  • have it deleted (see above), or its use restricted;
  • object to the use based on our legitimate interest (logs and backups).

Write to us at pavelbonev@gmail.com. We answer within one month. Since we keep no e-mail or name, we may ask you to confirm the request from inside the app, signed in to the account it is about.

You can also complain to the Bulgarian data protection authority, the Commission for Personal Data Protection (2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592, www.cpdp.bg), or to the authority of the EU country where you live.

Children

Euromondo is not meant for children under 14. If we learn that a child under 14 has an account, we delete it. A parent can write to us to ask for that.

Security

The apps talk to the server only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only we have access to the server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. If Euromondo ever starts to collect anything new, this page will say so before that version of the app is released.
