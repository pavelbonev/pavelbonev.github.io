Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Applies to the NEARena app for Android (com.simplia.nearena)

In short: We collect no personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads and no tracking, and apart from Google Play's purchase the app sends nothing over the internet.

What stays on your phone

These things are kept only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We never receive them:

  • your nickname and a random player ID the app makes the first time it starts;
  • the people you have played with, your favourites and your wins against them;
  • your settings (bot level, board options and the like) and your free Pro tries.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of this data in your Google account, so that it comes back on a new phone. You decide that in your phone's settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the data from the phone.

Bluetooth

  • Bluetooth is used only to connect the phones in a room, directly to each other.
  • While you host a room, phones nearby that look for rooms can see your nickname and a short code made from your player ID, so that friends recognise you before they join.
  • The phones in a room exchange the nicknames, player IDs and moves of the game. Nothing else is sent, and nothing leaves the room.
  • On Android 8 to 11 the system shows a location permission, because Android needs it to search for Bluetooth devices. NEARena does not use, read or store your location. On Android 12 and later the app does not ask for location at all.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Purchases

NEARena Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your name, e-mail or payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been bought. Google Play keeps your purchase history (whether and when you bought Pro), and Google's purchase library in the app sends Google diagnostic data about the purchase, under the same policy. None of it reaches us.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: Settings → Apps → NEARena → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This deletes everything the app keeps.
  • The copy in Android backup: in the backup settings of your Google account (on most phones Settings → Google → Backup).
  • Your purchase history: Google keeps it; you can see and manage it at myaccount.google.com.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you write to us, we will confirm that.

Children

NEARena collects no personal data from anyone, children included. There are no ads, no chat and no way to contact strangers: you play only with phones in the same room.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to the e-mail address shown on NEARena's page in Google Play.
