How it works

Sign in

Sign in with Google and choose a nickname. We do not ask for your name or e-mail.

Predict

Give your score for each match. You can change it as often as you like until kick-off.

Follow it live

Results, group tables and the standings of the players change live while the matches are played.

How the points work

3

Exact score

You guessed the result exactly.

1

Right outcome

Win, draw or loss, but not the exact score.

10

The champion

You picked the team that wins the tournament.

Only the result after the regular 90 minutes counts. You pick the champion until the knockout stage starts. When two players have the same points, the one with more correct predictions (exact scores and right outcomes together) ranks higher, and after that the one who made fewer predictions.

Everything in the app

The same on Android and in the browser, in seven languages.

Matches and live results

Every match of the tournament with its result, stadium and city. Search for a team, or show only one group, the upcoming, the finished or the live matches.

Results, tables and standings update by themselves while the matches are played, without refreshing.

Your prediction

Set the score with a few taps. Next to it you see the chances from the teams' Elo ratings, the most likely scores and how all players have predicted the match.

Your prediction appears at once on your other devices.

The standings

All players, page by page, with your own place one tap away: points, predictions, exact scores and outcomes.

Tap a player to send them a message.

With the subscription

Swarms

A swarm is your own group: friends, family or colleagues. Create one with a name and a PIN, give the PIN to your friends, and you have your own standings and your own chat.

You can be in several swarms. Whoever creates a swarm can edit it and remove members.

The bracket

The whole tournament as one picture you can zoom. In the app it is under “Streams”.

Groups

The table of every group, with the teams that go through.

Knockout stage

Every round up to the final, with its matches and results.

The champion

Pick the team that will win, with each team's chances and the picks of the other players. Your 10 points count while your team is in the tournament and are lost if it is knocked out.

Team statistics

Matches, wins, draws, losses, goals and averages for every team.

Past tournaments

Every past World Cup and Euro in the app, with its matches, groups and standings as they were.

Chat

Direct messages with any player, and a chat for each swarm.

Floating score

On Android: a small window with the score of a match that stays over your other apps.

Free, with one exception

Everything in Euromondo is free and without ads. Only swarms need the subscription.

You buy it in Google Play or the App Store, where the exact price with VAT is shown. It renews every year until you cancel it in the store. Bought on one device, it works wherever you sign in to the same account, also in the browser, where it is not sold.

A game, not betting

In Euromondo you never stake money and there are no prizes. The chances and likely scores are statistics from the teams' ratings, for fun, and not advice for betting.
