Questions and answers

How do I sign in?

With your Google account. We get only a number for your account, not your name or e-mail. Then choose a nickname; you can change it at any time in the menu with your nickname.

Until when can I change my prediction?

Until the match starts. After kick-off the prediction is locked. The champion can be picked until the knockout stage starts.

How are the points counted?

3 points for the exact score, 1 point for the right outcome (win, draw or loss), 10 points for the champion. Only the result after the regular 90 minutes counts. With the same points, the player with more correct predictions (exact scores and right outcomes together) ranks higher, and after that the one who made fewer predictions.

Why do I get no notifications?

Euromondo sends no push notifications. Results and messages arrive live while the app or the web app is open.

Someone writes offensive things to me. What can I do?

Write to us with the player's nickname and what they wrote. We remove offensive messages and nicknames.

Do swarms cost anything?

No. Swarms are free for everyone, like the rest of Euromondo. If you bought the old subscription, it is not renewed and runs out on its own; there is nothing to cancel.

How do I delete my account?

In the app or in the web app: open the menu with your nickname and choose “Delete account”. Step by step: Delete your account.
