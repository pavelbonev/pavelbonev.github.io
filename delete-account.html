Delete your Euromondo account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app or in the browser. It takes a minute and cannot be undone.

How

Android

Open Euromondo, open the menu with your nickname, choose “Delete account” and confirm.

Browser

Open the web app, sign in, open the menu with your nickname, choose “Delete account” and confirm.

If you no longer have the app, use the web app: it works in any browser, with the same account. If you cannot sign in any more, write to pavelbonev@gmail.com with your nickname, and we will help you.

What is deleted

  • your sign-in IDs and your nickname;
  • your predictions and champion picks, also from past tournaments, and your points and statistics;
  • your swarm memberships: a swarm you created passes to the member who joined it first, and a swarm without other members is deleted;
  • the records of your subscription.

What stays

  • your chat messages, shown as from “deleted user” and no longer linked to you (write to us if you want some of them removed);
  • copies in our backups, which disappear within 12 months;
  • the purchase history at Google or Apple, which they keep under their own policies.

Your subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play or in your Apple Account; otherwise the store keeps renewing it every year.
